# About CURA

CURA gives care home operators a single platform to manage residents, staff, care plans, and compliance — so teams can focus on resident care instead of admin work.

## Our story
CURA started after our founders spent a year shadowing care home managers who were running 24/7 operations on paper logs, whiteboards, and a patchwork of spreadsheets. Medication rounds, incident reports, and CQC evidence all lived in different places — pulling it together for an inspection could take days.

We built CURA to put resident care, staff scheduling, and compliance reporting in one system that works the way a real shift works: fast, mobile-friendly, and built around the people actually using it, not just the people buying it.

Today CURA supports care homes and multi-site providers who need to trust their data, pass inspections with confidence, and give staff more time back for the work that matters most — caring for residents.

## What we believe
- **Care comes first** — every feature exists to give staff more time with residents, not less.
- **Trust by default** — compliance and data security are built in, not bolted on after the fact.
- **Built with operators** — we build alongside registered managers and care staff, not just for them.
- **Simple, fast, reliable** — software that works during a busy shift, not just in a product demo.
