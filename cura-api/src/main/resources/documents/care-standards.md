# Care Standards and Quality

## Regulatory Framework

All Cura care homes operate in compliance with the Care Quality Commission (CQC) fundamental standards. Our homes are inspected regularly and inspection reports are available on the CQC website and at reception.

## Care Planning

Each resident has an individual care plan that is reviewed at least monthly, or sooner if their needs change. Care plans are developed in partnership with the resident and their family or representative. They cover:
- Personal care and hygiene
- Nutrition and hydration
- Medication management
- Mobility and falls prevention
- Social activities and mental stimulation
- End-of-life wishes (where appropriate)

## Staffing

Minimum staffing levels are maintained at all times in line with CQC requirements. All staff undergo enhanced DBS checks before employment. Mandatory training includes safeguarding, manual handling, infection control, dementia awareness, and medication management.

## Medication Management

Medications are administered only by trained and competency-assessed staff. Medication Administration Records (MARs) are completed for every dose. Any errors or near-misses are reported and reviewed as part of our clinical governance process.

## Safeguarding

We have a zero-tolerance policy towards abuse, neglect, or exploitation of residents. All staff are trained to recognise and report safeguarding concerns. Concerns are referred to the Local Authority Designated Officer (LADO) and CQC as required.

## Activities and Wellbeing

Each home employs a dedicated activities coordinator. A weekly programme of individual and group activities is provided, including reminiscence, music, gentle exercise, arts and crafts, and trips out. Activities are tailored to resident preferences and abilities.

## Complaints and Feedback

Complaints can be made verbally, in writing, or via the Cura platform. All complaints are acknowledged within two working days and investigated within 28 days. If residents or families are dissatisfied with the outcome, they may refer to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman.

## Privacy and Dignity

We uphold the right of every resident to be treated with respect, courtesy, and dignity. Staff knock before entering rooms, use preferred names, and support residents to make their own choices wherever possible.
