# CURA Pricing & Packages

## Cura Essential — $299/mo ($2,990/yr)
For single care homes starting digital operations.
- Resident profiles and care notes
- Medication and appointment reminders
- Daily occupancy and unit tracking
- Email support

Not included: multi-site dashboard, CQC report export, API access.

## Cura Plus — $699/mo ($6,990/yr)
For growing teams that need operational visibility.
- Everything in Essential
- Shift and hours oversight
- Multi-facility analytics dashboards
- Priority support

Not included: API access.

## Cura Enterprise — Custom pricing
For regional providers with advanced governance needs.
- Everything in Plus
- Advanced permissions and audit exports
- Custom onboarding and integrations
- Dedicated success manager

Enterprise pricing depends on facility count, integrations, and governance requirements — talk to sales for a quote.

## Pricing FAQs

**What happens after my 14-day trial ends?**
You'll be prompted to pick a plan before losing access. Nothing is billed automatically during the trial, and there's no card required to start it.

**Can I switch plans later?**
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

**Do you charge per resident or per facility?**
Pricing is per facility, not per resident, so growing occupancy doesn't increase your bill.

**Is there a setup fee?**
No setup fee on Essential or Plus. Enterprise includes custom onboarding scoped to your rollout.

**What does "Custom" pricing mean for Enterprise?**
Enterprise pricing depends on facility count, integrations, and governance requirements — talk to sales for a quote.
