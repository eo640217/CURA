# CURA Homepage FAQs

## Getting started

**How long does onboarding take?**
Most facilities are live in 2 to 4 weeks, including setup, training, and migration support.

**Do I need to migrate existing resident records?**
Our onboarding team helps import resident, unit, and care plan data from spreadsheets or your previous system before go-live.

**Is training included?**
Yes. All plans include staff onboarding sessions; Plus and Enterprise include ongoing refresher training.

## Access & roles

**Can residents and employees both sign in?**
Yes. Cura supports role-based access so residents, staff, and administrators each see tools relevant to them.

**Can I limit what staff can see across facilities?**
Admins can scope staff accounts to specific facilities or units, and restrict sensitive actions like resident deletion to admin roles.

**Do you support multiple facilities under one account?**
Yes. Cura Plus and Enterprise include multi-site operational oversight and centralized reporting across all your facilities.

## Pricing & billing

**What happens after my 14-day trial ends?**
You'll be prompted to pick a plan before losing access. Nothing is billed automatically during the trial, and there's no card required to start it.

**Can I switch plans later?**
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

**Do you charge per resident or per facility?**
Pricing is per facility, not per resident, so growing occupancy doesn't increase your bill.

## Security & compliance

**Is CURA GDPR compliant?**
Yes, CURA is fully GDPR compliant, with data processing agreements available for every plan.

**Are you ISO 27001 certified?**
Yes. Our infrastructure and data handling practices are ISO 27001 certified and independently audited annually.

**Does CURA support CQC reporting?**
Yes. Cura Plus and Enterprise generate CQC-ready reports automatically, so you stay audit-ready without a last-minute scramble.

## Support

**What support comes with each plan?**
Essential includes email support, Plus includes priority support with faster response times, and Enterprise includes a dedicated success manager.

**Do you offer phone support?**
Phone support is available on Plus and Enterprise plans; Essential customers reach us by email.
