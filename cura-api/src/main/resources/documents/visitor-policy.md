# Visitor Policy

## Visiting Hours

Visitors are welcome every day between 10:00 and 20:00. We encourage regular visits as they support resident wellbeing. Outside of standard hours, visits can be arranged by contacting the home manager at least 24 hours in advance.

## Signing In

All visitors must sign in at reception on arrival and sign out on departure. A valid form of photo identification may be requested on first visit. Visitors are issued a visitor badge which must be worn at all times.

## Children

Children are welcome and encouraged to visit. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult at all times. Please let reception know if you are bringing children so that appropriate areas can be prepared.

## Infection Control

Visitors are asked to use the hand sanitiser provided at the entrance and at resident room entrances. During periods of heightened infection risk (such as seasonal flu or outbreak management), additional restrictions may apply. The home will communicate any changes by phone and email.

## Bringing Items for Residents

Gifts and personal items are welcome. All food brought from outside the home must be disclosed to the care team due to dietary and allergy requirements. Alcohol may only be given to residents with prior agreement from the care team.

## End-of-Life Visiting

We operate an open visiting policy for residents receiving end-of-life care. Families may stay at any time, including overnight. Please speak to the nurse in charge to make arrangements.

## Complaints During a Visit

If a visitor has a concern during a visit, please speak to the senior carer or nurse on duty. Formal complaints can be made in writing to the Home Manager or via the Cura platform.
