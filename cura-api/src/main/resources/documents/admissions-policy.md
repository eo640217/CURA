# Admissions Policy

## Eligibility

Cura care homes accept residents who require residential or nursing care. Admission is based on a pre-admission assessment completed by our care team. We accept residents aged 65 and over, as well as younger adults with complex care needs.

## Waiting List

If a room is unavailable, prospective residents are placed on a waiting list. Families are contacted within 48 hours of availability. Priority is given based on clinical urgency and date of registration.

## Assessment Process

Before admission, a needs assessment is carried out by a senior nurse or care coordinator. The assessment covers:
- Medical history and current conditions
- Mobility and personal care requirements
- Cognitive and mental health needs
- Social and emotional wellbeing
- Dietary and nutritional requirements

## Documentation Required

On or before the day of admission, the following must be provided:
- GP referral letter
- Medical records and medication list
- NHS number
- Next of kin contact details
- Power of attorney documentation (if applicable)
- Identification (passport or driving licence)

## Trial Period

All new residents undertake a four-week settling-in period. During this time, the care plan is reviewed and adjusted. Either party may end the placement during this period with seven days' notice.

## Fees

Fees are agreed in advance and set out in the Resident Agreement. We accept self-funding, local authority funding, NHS Continuing Healthcare, and third-party top-up arrangements. A full fee schedule is available on request.
